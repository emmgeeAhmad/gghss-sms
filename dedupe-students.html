Find & Clean Up Duplicate Students

Scans every student record and groups any that share the same admission number, or (if admission number is blank) the same name and class. For each group, pick which one to keep — the tool will automatically copy over any filled-in fields from the others before removing them, so you don't lose a father's name or DOB that only exists on the duplicate.

Nothing is changed until you review every group and click Apply. This also re-sorts the full student list by class, section and name, so the roster stops looking haphazard.
